The subscription
you forgot you had

Most Australians stop using their public library when they leave school. For 18 to 34 year olds who did: what a free library card gets you today, and why it's worth picking back up.

1 – There's one near you

Did you know Australia has this many public libraries? Can you find the one closest to where you live?

1,409 public library branches across Australia in 2024–25, plus 90 mobile libraries

Joining is free, and one card works across every branch in your council area.

2 – Least likely to walk in

For most of us, the library was a school-years thing. Once we leave, 18 to 34 year olds become some of the least likely Australians to walk through a library door.

18% of 18–24 year olds visited a library in 2021–22, down from 28% of 15–17 year olds
28.8% of Australians hold a library card, fewer than 3 in 10

The rest of this page shows what everyone else is missing.

3 – It's not just you

The whole country drifted away from the library through the 2010s, as paid subscriptions became the default way to read, watch and listen.

8.1 → 5.5 items borrowed per Australian a year, 2010–11 to 2019–20
−32% fall in borrowing per person in the Northern Territory over the decade, the steepest of any state. Tasmania and the ACT were close behind.

4 – The library is in your pocket

You don't even have to go in. With a free card, apps like Libby, BorrowBox and Hoopla let you borrow ebooks and audiobooks on the train, in bed, or anywhere else.

61 million ebooks, audiobooks and streams borrowed in 2024–25, nearly three times as many as in 2018–19

No queues and no late fees: digital loans return themselves when they're due.

Find your state

Ebooks were barely 2% of loans in 2014–15. Slide through the years to watch digital borrowing spread, then click your state.

5 – A free place to study, work and meet

Libraries are also some of the last free places to sit, study and get online.

938,000 hours of library study and meeting rooms booked in 2024–25, nearly double 2021–22

They also ran 434,000 free programs, from study help and coding to writing workshops.

Your library card is free, it fits in your phone, and there is almost certainly a branch near you. Find yours.
