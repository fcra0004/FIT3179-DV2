The Subscription
You Forgot You Had!

Most Australians stop using their public library when they leave school. Find out what a free library card gets you today, and why you should consider picking one back up!

1 – What Are You Paying For?

Add up the services you pay for to read, listen and watch. A free library card already covers all of them!

$785 a year for ebooks, audiobooks, magazines and films on four popular apps, at current Australian prices.

Many Australians don't use their library card, or never even got one. Keep reading to find out what they're missing out on.

2 – Why Don't We Use It?

For most of us, the library was somewhere we went to during school. Once we leave, people aged 18 to 34 become some of the least likely Australians to walk through a library door.

18% of people aged 18 to 24 visited a library in 2021–22, down from 28% of those aged 15 to 17.
28.8% of Australians currently hold a library card (2024-25), fewer than 3 in 10.

3 – It's Not Just You

Australians began to drift away from the library through the 2010s as paid subscriptions became the default way to read, watch, and listen. Then COVID-19 only made things worse.

8.1 → 5.5 items borrowed per Australian a year, 2010–11 to 2019–20.
−32% fall in borrowing per person in the Northern Territory over the decade, the steepest of any state.

4 – A Library in Your Pocket!

You don't even have to go in. With a free card, apps like Libby, BorrowBox, and Hoopla let you borrow ebooks and audiobooks on the train, in bed, or anywhere else.

61 million ebooks, audiobooks and streams borrowed in 2024–25, nearly three times as many as in 2018–19.

In every state, at least 29% of loans are now digital. With no queues and no late fees, digital loans even return themselves when they're due!

Watch Your State Digitise!

5 – A Place to Study, Work and Meet

Libraries are also some of the last free places to sit, study, and get online.

938,000 hours of library study and meeting rooms booked in 2024–25, nearly double 2021–22.

They also ran 434,000 free programs from study help and coding to writing workshops!
